Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This website is a personal portfolio. It has no user accounts, no database and no server of its own that collects your data. This page explains exactly what happens when you visit.

In short: no tracking cookies, no analytics, no advertising. If you contact me, your message goes through WhatsApp or your own email app, not through this website.

Who runs this site

piyushassudani.in is run by Piyush Chandra Prakash Assudani, Balotra, Rajasthan, India, founder of Assudani Group. For any privacy question, write to piyushassudani96@gmail.com.

Information this site collects

None directly. The site is a set of static files. There are no sign-ups, comment forms, analytics scripts or advertising pixels.

The contact form

The “Let’s build what’s next” form does not send anything to this website. When you press a button, your browser opens either WhatsApp (wa.me) or your email app, with your name and message already typed in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself from that app. From then on, the message is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own privacy policies, and I use it only to reply to you about your enquiry.

What is stored in your browser

  • Time-of-day preview (pa-sky, session storage): if you choose a sky such as “Sunset” or “Night” in the header, that choice is kept for the current tab and cleared when you close it.
  • No cookies are set by this site.

Third parties involved

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of any web request. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider: like every web host, the server that delivers these files may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser, time, page requested) for security and reliability.
  • External links: client websites, Google Play, WhatsApp, GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram are separate services with their own policies. Leaving this site through a link means their policy applies.

Your choices and rights

Because this site does not hold personal data, there is usually nothing to access or delete here. If you have sent me a message and want it deleted, or want to know what I hold from our conversation, email me and I will act on it. Indian users have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and I will respect them.

Children

This site is not directed at children and knowingly collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if analytics are ever added), this page will be updated and the date above changed.
